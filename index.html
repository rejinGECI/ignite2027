About Go Live Date

What does this do?

  • All student streaks will only count days from the go-live date onwards
  • Statistics and progress tracking will ignore any data before this date
  • This ensures fair tracking when the system launches mid-semester or mid-year
  • You can change this date at any time, and all calculations will update accordingly
